# Licensing terms: publisher display and the assistant feed

Draft standard clauses for every publisher licence, written 3 October 2026, before any assistant deal. Points marked [COUNSEL] need a lawyer's view before the first signature. Points marked [DECIDE] are commercial choices not yet made.

## 1. Two channels

The same editions reach readers through two channels.

**Publisher display.** A licensed publisher shows an edition to readers under its own name: its site, app, newsletter, audio, print.

**Assistant feed.** yous.news supplies editions and their archive to AI assistants, search products and other machine readers, which answer questions from them.

## 2. Exclusive display, non-exclusive feed

2.1 The publisher holds the exclusive right to display the editions in its licensed area under a publication name. yous.news will not licence the same editions to another publisher for display in that area while the licence is in force.

2.2 The publisher grants nothing exclusive over the assistant feed. yous.news may supply the same editions, and their archive, to assistant products in any territory.

2.3 yous.news owns the editions it writes. Corrections, edits and additions the publisher's editors make belong to the publisher and are never carried in the assistant feed unless the publisher agrees in writing.

## 3. The publisher credit in the assistant feed

3.1 While the credit is active (clause 4), every item from the licensed area in the assistant feed carries the publisher credit, and assistant licensees must show it with the item, unaltered.

3.2 Where no credit is active, the item is credited to yous.news alone.

## 4. The credit lasts only while the publisher publishes

4.1 The credit is active only while both are true:

- the licence is in force and paid, and
- the publisher is displaying the editions to readers.

4.2 The credit lapses when the licence ends for any reason, or when the publisher has not displayed the editions for 14 consecutive days.

4.3 Within 7 days of a lapse, yous.news removes the publisher's name from every item in the assistant feed, past items in the archive included, and tells assistant licensees to stop showing it.

4.4 A publisher that resumes, or a new licence for the area, restores the credit from the restart date. Items from the gap stay credited to yous.news alone.

4.5 The publisher tells yous.news within 7 days if it stops displaying the editions. yous.news may check display at any time. [DECIDE: whether to add an automated check that a sample of each day's stories appears on the publisher's own pages.]

## 5. What the credit may say

5.1 The credit is the publisher's publication name as it appears on its masthead, for example "La Repubblica Milano" or "Vorarlberger Nachrichten".

5.2 It may contain no more than 40 characters including spaces.

5.3 It may not contain a URL or domain, a slogan or tagline, a price, an offer, a phone number, an emoji, a call to action, or the name of a product, advertiser or sponsor.

5.4 One credit per licensed area. yous.news approves the credit before it goes live and may refuse or shorten one that breaks this clause.

5.5 The publisher may not ask for a link, logo or extra text to be carried with the credit in the assistant feed. [DECIDE: whether to allow a single link to the publisher's own page for the same edition, which assistants may or may not show.]

## 6. Held and edited stories

6.1 If the publisher's editors hold a story, that story never appears in the assistant feed with the publisher's credit.

6.2 yous.news decides separately whether a held story appears in the assistant feed at all. If it does, it carries the yous.news credit alone and goes through yous.news's own checks first. [COUNSEL: whether a story a publisher held for legal reasons should be withheld from the assistant feed entirely while the hold stands.]

6.3 The publisher credit attaches to the yous.news version of a story. It never implies the publisher wrote, edited or approved that version, and assistant licensees may not say it did. [COUNSEL: wording assistants must use, for example "Local edition carried by La Repubblica Milano".]

## 7. Money

7.1 The assistant feed earns the publisher no fee by default; the credit is the consideration. [DECIDE: a revenue share on assistant income from the publisher's area, which would make the clause easier to accept and cost little at the start.]

## 8. Assistant licences must carry the same rules

Every assistant licence obliges the assistant to: show the credit as supplied; stop showing it within 7 days of notice; never alter or extend it; never present a credited publisher as the author of the item. A breach lets yous.news suspend the feed.

## 9. Notes for the feed (to build with the assistant feed)

- Each edition carries a credit record: name, start date, end date, status. The feed reads it at request time, so a lapse takes effect everywhere at once.
- Validate the credit against clause 5 in code: length, no URL, no digits beyond a year, no emoji.
- The credit is never written into story text, only into a separate field, so removing it never means rewriting archived stories.
