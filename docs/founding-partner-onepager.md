# Flaneur for Real Estate Partners

**A daily newsletter for your neighborhood, branded as yours, delivered to your
clients every morning.**

## The idea

Flaneur publishes a daily neighborhood briefing - local news, openings, events,
market notes - for 270 neighborhoods across 42 countries, every morning at
7 AM local time.

For real estate, we do something specific: **one broker per neighborhood.**
That broker becomes the exclusive partner. The daily email goes out under their
name, photo, and brokerage, carrying their listings, to their own client list.

## What you get

- **Your brand on a daily touchpoint.** 365 mornings a year, your past clients
  and prospects open an email that is yours: your name in the "from" line, your
  photo, your listings.
- **Exclusive territory.** One agent per neighborhood. Your competitor cannot
  have it.
- **Zero daily work.** Flaneur writes and sends it. You do nothing each
  morning, and you receive a copy yourself so you always see what your clients
  see.
- **Your list stays yours.** You bring your client emails and you keep every
  contact. A personal subscribe link lets you grow the list over time.
- **A weekly performance report**, every Monday.

## What it costs

**Free for founding partners.** Flaneur is in beta. Founding partners join at
no cost, with no card required. If pricing is introduced later, the
founding-partner rate is locked in.

## Why it matters

A single luxury print ad runs $5,000 to $20,000 and appears once. A social
media agency retainer is $2,000 to $5,000 a month. This is 365 branded
touchpoints a year with the clients you already have, for free while we are in
beta. And your competitor cannot have your neighborhood.

## How to start

Setup takes about five minutes at **readflaneur.com/partner**: choose your
neighborhood, add your details and a headshot, paste your client list, send
yourself a preview, then activate. The first branded edition goes out the next
morning at 7 AM local time.

See a live sample of the underlying content: **readflaneur.com/new-york/tribeca**
